# Auterion Design System — Build Guide

**AD-2026-001 · v1.1 · 2026-08-03 · Owner: Yasen Dimov**

## What changed in v1.1, and why

v1.0 was written for a blank slate — *"Auxiliary and Flight Manual are reference material, not
foundations."* That premise was roughly a year out of date. This repo was already at ROADMAP
Phase 7 with seven packages, ~36 tested Vue primitives, a four-tier token model, a bidirectional
Figma bridge, and five drift gates in CI.

Executed literally, v1.0's Weeks 6–12 would have rebuilt working, gated infrastructure and arrived
somewhere **less** capable: a three-tier token model against the shipped GTC four-tier, a sketched
`scheme × context × register` against the shipped and orthogonality-gated `theme × register`.
v1.0's own Appendix A conceded the point; its sequencing never absorbed the concession.

So v1.1 keeps what v1.0 got right — the doctrine, the team model, the governance — replaces the
90-day build with a delta plan against the repo as it stands, and hands execution sequencing to
[`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md), which is and remains the single forward-looking source of truth. This
document is doctrine, org, and governance. It does not schedule component work.

## Part I — Doctrine

Five rules. Every later step applies one of them. Unchanged from v1.0 except where noted.

**1. Decisions before pixels.** A world-class system is a stack of ratified decisions, each with a
rationale and an owner. The failure mode was exploration without ratification. *Fixed in v1.1:*
[`decisions/`](decisions/) is live — 19 entries, format-gated by `pnpm decisions:check`. A decision
not in the log does not exist.

**2. Code is the source of truth; Figma is a mirror.** Tokens and components live in the repo
first. Figma consumes tokens and mirrors shipped components; it never defines them. Exploration
tools stay in the loop, but their output enters the system only by being rebuilt in code and
ratified. This is the only architecture that survives a one-person design team. (`AD-D-022`)

**3. One system, two registers.** Brand/editorial and operational surfaces come from one token
source. These are registers of one language, not two systems. The operational register carries
regulatory weight — status colour misuse in a ground-control station is a safety issue — so it is
governed by standards, not taste. (`AD-D-020`, `AD-D-014`)

**4. The brand is the strategy made visible.** *Build swarms, not drones*; open architecture;
autonomous mass; human-in-the-loop; aligned with democracies. The language must read **trusted,
mission-critical, precise**, and must differ from both consumer-drone playfulness and defense-prime
cliché.

**5. Small surface, hard edges.** A deliberately small system with unbreakable invariants beats a
large one with soft ones. Expansion is cheap once the invariants hold; retrofitting invariants onto
a large system is what kills design systems.

Unchanged from v1.0, with one correction: `.claude/docs/phase-7-autonomy-multi-agent-plan.md`
already covers part of the Q1-2027 operational layer.

**Q4 2026 — Depth.** Component set to ~30 (virtualized DataTable, forms, navigation). Imagery

**Q1 2027 — The operational layer.** The entity atom: one typed entity backing map marker, roster
row, and inspector panel. Affiliation as its own token axis per MIL-STD-2525E/APP-6(D),
shape-redundant, never overloading status. Data-freshness axis. K-of-N collective health. External
conformance review — standards compliance is never self-certified. `GuardedAction` already ships.

**Q2 2027 — The brand book & launch.** Guidelines published *from* the system, not as a parallel
PDF. Site, decks, and product surfaces visibly one language. Retrospective + v1.0 tag.

**Continuous:** quarterly device review — new patterns admitted only with a rendered specimen and a
written grant; unused devices retired.

## Part IV — The team

One designer, ten agents. **The roster is [`design-team/roster.md`](design-team/roster.md)** — that
file is the source of truth, and `.claude/agents/`, `design-team/dist/personas.md` and
`.crystl/heroes/` are all generated from it, drift-gated by `pnpm design-team:check`.

*Corrected in v1.1:* v1.0 assumed one team. There were two — the five crystl heroes in `AGENTS.md`
and the ten roster roles — with overlapping ownership of `packages/tokens` and
`packages/vue/src/primitives`. Resolved: subagents primary, crystl kept only for parallel isolated
worktrees, `AGENTS.md` rewritten to point at the roster.

**The second-reader rule is the load-bearing trick:** every substantive output is critiqued by one
other agent before it reaches you. You review conclusions, not drafts. The routing table and the
write-scope table live in the roster.

**Standing instructions**, now generated into every persona: cite decision IDs rather than
restating rationale; propose entries at `status: proposed` and never ratify; rank your evidence on
the roster's ladder and never call "verified" what was only inferred; do not invent an unknown.

**Operating rhythm.** Monday: a 5–10 line brief, dispatched. Daily: agents run async, batched into
sessions. Friday (60 min): crit — the Design Director assembles the week into one critique document
with proposed log entries; you ratify or bounce. Per gate: human review.

**Decision rights.** Agents propose, you decide, your boss ratifies gates. Three permanent
escalations: anything touching the operational register's safety semantics, purchases above your
budget line, and anything customer-visible before launch. Everything else is yours — the system
dies if routine calls wait on meetings.

## Part VI — Governance & quality

**The decision log** is the constitution: [`decisions/`](decisions/). Immutable once ratified
(superseded, never edited). Agents cite entries; a proposal that contradicts a ratified entry must
name it and argue for supersession. `pnpm decisions:check` gates format and index.

**The quality gate**, cumulative: token purity and the 13 GTC rules · contrast pairs · naming lint ·
axe per component (status never colour-alone, focus visible) · **visual regression per theme ×
register** · type checks · zero-warning lint · coverage floor · five drift gates (icons, brand,
docs props, design team, decision log) · `pack:smoke`. The gate precedes whatever it guards.

**Versioning:** changesets, semver, pre-1.0 until the Q2 2027 launch (`AD-D-035`). Breaking token
renames ship with a codemod or an alias layer *after* 1.0.

**Standards register (operational surfaces):** MIL-STD-1472 (status hierarchy, `AD-D-014`),
IEC 62288 (alert model), MIL-STD-2525E / APP-6(D) (affiliation symbology), WCAG 2.2 AA minimum with
AAA targets for body text. Reviewed externally before anything operational ships.

**Anti-drift:** one accent moment per surface as a reviewable rule · new devices need specimen +
grant · quarterly retirement of unused patterns · the "admired, not adopted" list re-read before
adding any new inspiration.
