demo(eco): the specimen sheet argues against the shipped artifact

The One Auterion page now imports `IdentityBar` and `Launcher` from
`@auxiliary/shell` instead of drawing its own. Every claim below the cover is
therefore a claim about the package.

- **A new section — the third axis.** Theme and register have controls; input
  modality does not, because an operator does not choose their pointer. Four
  specimens of one bar with only `[data-input]` and `[data-register]` changing,
  showing the 44px touch floor composing *over* register density rather than
  replacing it — the real rugged-controller case. What moves (hit areas) and
  what does not (tracking, rules, tab padding) is the point.
- **The build plan states where it has got to.** Each step carries a `shipped` /
  `drafted` / `next` state on the row rather than in the markup, so a caption
  cannot claim a step that did not land.
- **The blue question is answered, with what answering it turned up** — the
  gamut-boundary finding and why the semantic `brand` role does not move.
- **The measured bar height reads the token tier**, not a page-local class, so
  it also proves the register axis still reaches the component tier: two equal
  numbers would mean it had stopped.

Craft, found by looking at it rather than at the code:

- The launcher's section heading was the same size as its nine tile titles, so
  the grid had no head. It gets its own rank.
- The Simulation glyph — a solid triangle over a dashed rule — read as the
  letter **A** at 24px, directly beside the Auterion mark, which is an angular
  A. Redrawn as a vehicle inside a dashed ring, which rhymes with Control's
  solid reticle on purpose: Simulation deliberately declares Control's theme and
  register, and the dash is the whole difference.
